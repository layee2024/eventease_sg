# G9T6 WADII Group Project (EventEase SG)
EventEase SG is an online event discovery and planning website created for Singapore students in tertiary institutions. Students do not have time to monitor the great range of cultural, recreational, and social events, and therefore miss chances or are stuck between the options while planning. EventEase SG took that place by consolidating event information, offering them personal suggestions, crowd-powered real-time updates, and easy planning features. The site intends to enable students to discover exciting activities, organize last-minute but stress-less activities, and share their stories.
